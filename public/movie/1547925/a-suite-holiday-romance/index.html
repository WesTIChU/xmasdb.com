2025Premiered: 2025-12-13★ 6.9 / 10 on TMDB

Synopsis

During Christmas week, Sabrina jumps at the opportunity to ghostwrite a memoir for a famous art dealer at the iconic Grand Fairbanks Hotel in New York City. The week gets complicated when she meets a handsome British visitor at the hotel bar; she thinks he’s a member of the British aristocracy, and he thinks she’s a wealthy hotel guest.
