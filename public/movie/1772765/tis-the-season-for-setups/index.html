'Tis the Season for Setups

Synopsis

Hyper organized single mom and party planner Lexi, her free-spirited mother June, and teen daughter Piper are set to spend Christmas at a local ski resort after Lexi is hired to organize their annual holiday party. Upon arrival, she immediately butts heads with Ridge, the ski instructor and son of the lodge's owner, Dan.
