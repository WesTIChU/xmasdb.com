Snow Globe Town

UPCOMING2026Premiered: 2026-12-12

Synopsis

Emily Church joins her sister Zoe and Zoe’s longtime boyfriend on a Christmas getaway to Pine Grove, a town famous for its romantic holiday traditions. While helping with Zoe’s proposal plans, Emily is paired with skeptical journalist Bert Baxter. As they are drawn into the town’s festive celebrations, unexpected feelings begin to challenge what they both thought they knew about love, family and Christmas.
