Return to Santa

UPCOMING2026Premiered: 2026-11-27

Synopsis

Decades after a childhood letter to Santa goes unanswered, Jake has grown into a practical adult who no longer believes in Christmas magic. When Santa finally discovers the lost letter and begins granting Jake’s long-forgotten wishes, his orderly life is thrown into chaos. With his childhood best friend Julie unexpectedly back in town, the two work together to manage each new wish while growing closer along the way.

Cast & Characters

  • Paul Campbell as Jake
  • Rachel Boston as Julie
